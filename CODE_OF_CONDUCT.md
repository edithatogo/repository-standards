# Code of conduct

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and
other spaces maintained for repositories that adopt it. Participation should
allow people with different backgrounds and experience to contribute safely.

## Expected behaviour

Be respectful, evidence-oriented and privacy-conscious. Discuss ideas and code
without attacking people. Explain criticism with concrete examples, acknowledge
mistakes and make room for questions. Respect a person's request to end unwanted
contact and keep project discussions relevant to their purpose.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory conduct, threats, sexualised attention, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption and retaliation for a good-faith report are not
acceptable. Do not disclose another person's private information, credentials,
confidential records or unreported vulnerability details.

## Reporting

For content on GitHub, use the content menu's **Report content** action. Where
available, **Report to repository admins** sends a report to the maintainer;
**Report abuse to GitHub Support** is available for concerns covered by GitHub's
policies. GitHub documents the available reporting routes in
[Reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

If the reporting action is unavailable, use the GitHub Support contact route
linked from that guidance. Reports concerning the sole maintainer can be sent
to GitHub Support independently. Do not open a public issue containing private
evidence or identifying details of an incident. Provide relevant links and the
minimum context needed through the available private reporting channel.

Questions about this policy may be raised in a public issue only when they
contain no private incident details. Vulnerability reports follow the separate
[security policy](https://github.com/edithatogo/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Repository moderation is performed by the sole maintainer. Depending on the
conduct and available evidence, responses may include a clarification or
warning, editing or removing content, locking a discussion, restricting
participation, or referring abuse to GitHub. Serious or repeated incidents may
require an immediate restriction without preceding warnings.

The maintainer should consider context, explain moderation decisions where
practical and protect the reporter's privacy. Information is shared only as
needed to investigate, respond or meet platform obligations; absolute
confidentiality cannot be promised. A person seeking reconsideration should
provide relevant new information through an available private reporting route.
GitHub determines enforcement of its own platform policies.

These repositories do not have a separate moderation team or guaranteed response
time. This policy does not require a second maintainer or mandatory PR approval.
